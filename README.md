# Meme Maker

Make memes right on your Drawdy board: pick one of imgflip's 100 most popular templates or use your own picture, type the captions and place it. The meme remembers its captions, so when the joke changes you edit the meme instead of starting over.

![Opening Meme Maker, searching for drake, typing two captions on the Drake Hotline Bling template and placing the meme on the board.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-meme-maker/main/docs/media/meme-maker.gif)

## What it's for

- **Retros and brainstorms**: a meme says how the sprint went faster than a page of sticky notes.
- **Slides and workshops**: a joke between sections keeps the room with you.
- **Running jokes that stay editable**: change a caption or swap the template later, in place.

## How to use

1. Click the **Meme Maker** button on the right of the board.
2. Pick a template from the list, or type in **Search templates** to find one by name, such as "drake". To use your own picture, click **Upload a picture**, or drop or paste one anywhere in the panel.
3. Type your captions in the **Caption 1** and **Caption 2** boxes. The preview updates as you type; drag a caption's dashed box on the preview to move it.
4. Click **Place on board**. The meme lands in view, next to anything already there rather than on top of it.
5. To change it later, select the meme, click **Edit selected**, make your changes and click **Update**. **Place new** makes a copy instead, and **Stop editing** goes back to making new memes.

## Caption options

- **Text**: each caption has its own box. Long lines wrap to the caption's width, and a new line starts wherever you press Return. Captions are drawn in capitals in Anton, with an outline.
- **Size**: the slider sets the caption size from 24 to 160, in proportion to the picture.
- **Colour**: the **A** button swaps white text with a black outline for black text with a white outline.
- **Alignment**: left, centre or right within the caption's box.
- **Position**: drag the dashed box on the preview.
- **Add caption** adds another caption, up to six; **×** removes one.

## Tips

- ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z takes a meme back, from the panel too. A new meme is one undo step; an update takes two.
- Click a caption's dashed box and use the arrow keys to nudge it; hold Shift for bigger steps.
- **Change picture** picks another template or picture and keeps the captions you typed.
- If imgflip can't be reached, the panel shows the list it saved last time, with its date. Uploading your own picture works either way.
- Your 30 most recent uploaded pictures are kept in this browser, so memes made from them open again with **Edit selected**. In another browser the captions load and the panel asks you to drop the picture in again.

## Privacy

- The panel downloads the template list and the template pictures from imgflip.com, and the Anton font from jsDelivr.
- Pictures you upload stay in this browser and in the memes you place on your board. They are never sent to imgflip.
- Your last search is saved for next time. Captions live only in the memes you place.
