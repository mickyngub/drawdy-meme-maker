# Meme Maker (≧▽≦)

**say it with a meme.**

imgflip's 100 most popular templates, or your own picture, with bold outlined captions you drag into place. Drop one into a retro, a lesson or a launch board, and change the joke any time later ♡

![Opening Meme Maker, searching for drake, typing two captions on the Drake Hotline Bling template and placing the meme on the board.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-meme-maker/main/docs/media/meme-maker.gif)

---

## let's meme ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **grinning face** in the extension rail.
2. Search the templates, like *drake*, *boyfriend* or *brain*, and click one. Or click **Upload a picture**; dropping or pasting a picture into the panel works too.
3. Type your captions in **Caption 1** and **Caption 2**, then drag their dashed boxes on the preview to move them.
4. Click **Place on board**. To change it later, select it, click **Edit selected**, then **Update**.

> (・_・;) **can't find a template?** The list holds imgflip's 100 most popular memes right now; for any other, save the picture and click **Upload a picture**.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Your own picture**: upload, drop or paste one instead of a template.
- **Add caption**: up to six captions, anywhere on the picture.
- **Size**: the slider makes each caption bigger or smaller.
- **A**: swaps white text with a black outline for black text with a white outline.
- **Alignment**: left, centre or right within the caption's box.

---

## little things to try (っ˘ω˘ς )

- A retro board with one meme per column: what went well, what didn't, what's next.
- Drake on every "this or that" decision in a planning session.
- A photo from the team day out, with a caption only your team will get.
- **Change picture** to try the same joke on another template; your captions stay.
