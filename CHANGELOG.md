# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- the panel now uses Drawdy's own colours and font
- a proper drop zone for your own picture and a roomier template grid
- the caption editor has ink swatches, aligned icon buttons and a trash icon
- the place button greys out while there is nothing to place

## 1.0.0

First public release

- pick one of imgflip's 100 most popular meme templates, fetched live, and search them by name
- or upload, drop or paste your own picture; large pictures are shrunk to 1600 px on the long side
- a template starts with one caption per text box it has, up to six
- up to six captions, each with its own text, size, white or black colour and alignment, drawn in capitals in Anton with an outline
- drag captions on the preview, or nudge them with the arrow keys
- place the meme as an image beside existing content instead of on top of it, selected and in view
- select a placed meme to edit it in the panel and update it in place, keeping its spot, width, layer, frame, group, opacity and lock
- change the picture while keeping the captions you typed
- when imgflip can't be reached, the last saved template list shows with its date; uploads work either way
- the 30 most recent uploads are kept in this browser so memes made from them can be edited again; in another browser the captions load and the panel asks for the picture
- the last search is remembered; captions are not
- ⌘Z and Ctrl+Z undo on the board from the panel, Escape in a field leaves the field, and the panel follows the board theme while open
- storage and board permissions are asked when the panel first opens, not when the board loads
- a full board, a denied permission or a board that doesn't answer gets a clear message instead of a stuck button
